# Phase 2 - Research Project

## Chosen Topic
AI-Powered SEO Content Production

## Project Goal
To research how leading SEO professionals use artificial intelligence to create, oprimize and scale content production workflows.

## Repository Structure

This repository is organized to systematically document and manage AI-powered SEO research:

### **research/** - Main Research Directory
Contains all research materials, data collection, and analysis organized by content type and source format.

- **sources.md** - Comprehensive reference document containing a curated list of all sources used in the research, including links, authors, publication dates, and relevance notes. Serves as the bibliography for the entire project.

- **linkedin-posts/** - Collection of LinkedIn posts and discussions related to AI and SEO content production. This folder aggregates insights from industry professionals, trends, and community discussions that inform the research.

- **youtube-transcriptions/** - Transcribed content from YouTube videos about AI tools, SEO strategies, and content production workflows. Contains full transcripts of educational videos, tutorials, and expert interviews for easy reference and analysis.

- **other/** - Additional research materials including case studies, whitepapers, blog posts, and supplementary resources that don't fit into the other categories but contribute valuable insights to the project.

## Data Collection Methods

- **YouTube Transcripts** - Transcribed content from SEO experts and AI tool creators
- **LinkedIn Posts** - Industry discussions and trends from professionals
- **Expert Blogs** - Articles and guides from SEO and AI specialists
- **Case Studies** - Real-world AI applications in SEO content production
- **AI-Assisted Research** - Analysis using Claude and GitHub Copilot

## Research Methodology

1. Identify leading experts in AI-assisted SEO and content production.
2. Collect recent content from LinkedIn, YouTube, blogs, and podcasts.
3. Organize sources by platform and author.
4. Analyze recurring patterns, workflows, and recommendations.
5. Document findings for future reference and playbook development.
